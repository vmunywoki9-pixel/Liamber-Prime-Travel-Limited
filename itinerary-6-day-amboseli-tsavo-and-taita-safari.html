The journey

Experience the Best of Southern Kenya

This suggested six-day journey brings together three distinctive safari regions. Begin in Amboseli, known for its large elephant herds and views towards Mount Kilimanjaro when conditions are clear.

Continue through Tsavo West and Tsavo East, where wide open spaces, volcanic scenery and varied habitats create a memorable safari experience. Finish in the Taita Hills region, with time to enjoy its landscapes and wildlife surroundings.

Planning note: This is a sample itinerary. The final route, accommodation, park visits and travel times will be confirmed when your dates and preferences are discussed.
Your day-by-day journey

Suggested 6-Day Itinerary

A proposed route through Amboseli, Tsavo West, Tsavo East and the Taita Hills area.

DAY 01

Nairobi to Amboseli National Park

Depart Nairobi and travel towards Amboseli, enjoying the changing scenery along the way. Arrive in the area, check in at your selected accommodation and enjoy lunch, subject to the chosen lodge's schedule. Head out for an afternoon game drive.

  • Road transfer from Nairobi
  • Afternoon game drive, subject to arrival time
  • Overnight in the Amboseli area
DAY 02

Full Day in Amboseli

Spend the day exploring Amboseli's varied habitats. Look out for elephants, buffaloes, zebras, giraffes and other wildlife. Enjoy views of Mount Kilimanjaro when the weather is clear, with time for a relaxed break at your lodge or camp.

  • Morning game drive
  • Leisure and lunch at the lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Overnight in the Amboseli area
DAY 03

Amboseli to Tsavo West National Park

After breakfast, depart for Tsavo West. The journey introduces you to a different landscape, with rugged hills, volcanic features and natural springs. Depending on the route and park access arrangements, enjoy wildlife viewing en route and an afternoon exploration.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Transfer to Tsavo West
  • Afternoon exploration and game viewing
  • Overnight in or near Tsavo West
DAY 04

Tsavo West to Tsavo East

Enjoy a morning game drive before continuing towards Tsavo East. The two parks offer contrasting scenery and wildlife habitats. Arrive in the Tsavo East area and enjoy an afternoon game drive if time and park arrangements allow.

  • Morning game viewing
  • Transfer between the parks
  • Afternoon game drive, subject to timing
  • Overnight in or near Tsavo East
DAY 05

Tsavo East to Taita Hills

Enjoy an early game drive in Tsavo East before travelling towards the Taita Hills region. The area is known for its scenic highlands and wildlife surroundings. Settle into your selected accommodation and enjoy the remainder of the day at leisure or an arranged wildlife activity.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Transfer towards Taita Hills
  • Afternoon at leisure or arranged activity
  • Overnight in the Taita Hills area
DAY 06

Taita Hills to Nairobi

After breakfast, check out and begin the return journey to Nairobi. Your arrival time will depend on road conditions, the selected accommodation and any planned stops along the way.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Road transfer to Nairobi
  • Drop-off at an agreed location
