Discover the Mara

A Classic African Safari

The Maasai Mara is one of Kenya's best-known wildlife destinations. Its open grasslands, river systems and diverse habitats provide opportunities to encounter a variety of wildlife.

Game drives offer the chance to look for elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, zebras, antelopes, lions and other animals. Wildlife sightings vary with season, location and natural conditions.

We can discuss different trip durations, accommodation preferences and transport arrangements to help shape a safari around your travel plans.

Sample itinerary

Explore the Maasai Mara

This is a sample three-day safari outline. Exact departure times, accommodation, park arrangements and activities will be confirmed in your quotation.

Day01

Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Depart Nairobi for the Maasai Mara, travelling through the scenic Great Rift Valley. Arrive in the reserve area, check in at your selected accommodation and enjoy a game drive if timing and arrangements allow.

Nairobi • Great Rift Valley • Maasai Mara
Day02

Full Day Exploring the Mara

Spend the day exploring the reserve on game drives with opportunities to search for its varied wildlife and enjoy the landscapes. A picnic lunch or return to the lodge can be arranged according to the final programme.

Maasai Mara • Wildlife viewing • Game drives
Day03

Maasai Mara to Nairobi

After breakfast, depart the Mara and travel back towards Nairobi. Arrival time depends on road and travel conditions.

Maasai Mara • Return journey • Nairobi
