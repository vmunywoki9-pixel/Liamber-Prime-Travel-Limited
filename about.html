Who we are

Travel Beyond the Ordinary

Liamber Prime Travels Limited is a travel company based in Nairobi, Kenya, focused on helping travellers explore the country's remarkable destinations.

From the wildlife-rich plains of the Maasai Mara to the elephant country of Amboseli, the wilderness of Tsavo and the beautiful Kenyan coast, we aim to make discovering Kenya an enjoyable and well-planned experience.

We offer safari arrangements, holiday packages, excursions and tailor-made travel experiences designed around each traveller's interests, schedule and preferences.

Our approach to travel

Every Journey Begins with Your Story

We believe that travel should reflect the people taking the journey. Some travellers want an exciting wildlife safari, others prefer a relaxing coastal holiday, while some want to experience a little of everything.

Our approach begins by understanding your dates, group size, interests and preferred travel style. From there, we can discuss a suitable itinerary and provide a proposal for your consideration.
