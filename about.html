Our story

Travel that brings you closer to Kenya.

Liamber Prime Travels Limited is a Kenyan travel company focused on helping visitors discover the country's remarkable landscapes, wildlife, coast and cultural heritage.

From the open savannahs and iconic national parks to the Indian Ocean coastline and vibrant local communities, Kenya offers many ways to travel. We aim to make planning those experiences clear, personal and convenient.

Every traveller has different interests, available time and expectations. We listen to your plans and help you explore suitable travel options, from a short excursion to a multi-day safari or a holiday combining inland and coastal experiences.

What guides us

Our core values

The principles that shape how we plan journeys, communicate with travellers and engage with the places they visit.

Hospitality

We believe every traveller deserves a warm welcome, clear communication and attentive support throughout the planning process.

Sustainability

We encourage considerate travel that respects wildlife, natural habitats and the environment. Guests are encouraged to follow park rules and responsible wildlife-viewing guidance.

Community

We value meaningful connections with local people, culture and places, and recognise the role local communities play in Kenya's tourism experiences.

Travel with consideration

Our approach to positive travel

Tourism connects visitors with landscapes, wildlife, heritage and communities. We encourage our guests to make thoughtful choices and to respect the places and people they encounter.

Supporting local experiences

Where suitable, consider local guides, community experiences, locally made products and services that contribute to the destinations you visit.

Respecting wildlife

Observe animals responsibly, keep a safe distance, follow guide instructions and comply with the rules of parks and protected areas.

Caring for natural places

Help reduce waste, avoid disturbing habitats and leave natural and cultural sites as you found them.
