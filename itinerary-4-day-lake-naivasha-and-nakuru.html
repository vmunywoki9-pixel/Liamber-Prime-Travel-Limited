Two Lakes, Beautiful Landscapes and Wildlife

Lake Naivasha is a freshwater lake surrounded by scenic landscapes and rich birdlife. It is a popular destination for boat excursions and nature experiences.

Lake Nakuru National Park offers a different experience, with varied habitats and opportunities to see wildlife including rhinos, giraffes, buffaloes and other species.

This suggested four-day itinerary gives you time to experience both destinations at a comfortable pace. Activities and accommodation can be tailored to your travel preferences.

Your Safari Experience

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A suggested four-day programme. The final route and activities will be confirmed according to your departure point, accommodation and selected experiences.

Day01

Arrival in Naivasha

Depart for the Lake Naivasha area, enjoying views of the Great Rift Valley along the way. On arrival, check in at your selected accommodation and enjoy the surroundings. Depending on your arrival time, you may have an opportunity for a relaxed afternoon activity.

  • Travel to the Lake Naivasha area
  • Enjoy scenic Rift Valley views
  • Check in at your selected accommodation
  • Relax and enjoy the lakeside environment
Day02

Lake Naivasha Nature Experiences

Spend the day discovering the natural beauty of Lake Naivasha. An optional boat ride can offer opportunities to observe the lake's birdlife and see hippos in their natural habitat. Other activities may be arranged depending on your interests and the package selected.

  • Breakfast at your accommodation
  • Optional Lake Naivasha boat excursion
  • Birdwatching and lakeside photography
  • Optional additional nature activities
Day03

Transfer to Lake Nakuru National Park

After breakfast, travel towards Lake Nakuru National Park. Enjoy a game drive through the park's varied habitats, with opportunities to spot wildlife such as rhinos, Rothschild's giraffes, buffaloes, zebras and other animals. Later, proceed to your selected accommodation.

  • Breakfast and departure from Naivasha
  • Travel to Lake Nakuru National Park
  • Game drive and wildlife viewing
  • Overnight at selected accommodation
Day04

Morning Experience & Return Journey

Enjoy breakfast and, depending on your agreed schedule, an additional morning game drive or relaxed time at your accommodation. Check out and begin your return journey to the agreed drop-off point.

  • Breakfast at the accommodation
  • Optional morning game drive, subject to arrangements
  • Check-out and departure
  • Return transfer to your agreed destination
