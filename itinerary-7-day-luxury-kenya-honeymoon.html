A Journey for Two

Celebrate Love in the Heart of Kenya

Kenya offers a beautiful setting for a honeymoon, from the wildlife-rich plains of the Masai Mara to the scenic surroundings of Amboseli and the country's peaceful retreats.

This seven-day concept is designed for couples who want a balance of safari adventure, privacy and relaxation. Accommodation, pace and experiences can be selected to suit your preferred style of travel.

Whether you prefer intimate lodge stays, private game drives, scenic dining or a relaxed escape, our team can help shape a journey around your celebration.

Tailor-made experience: The route below is a suggested concept. Destinations, accommodation, activities and transfers will be finalised according to your dates, budget and preferences.
Your Romantic Escape

7-Day Suggested Itinerary

A sample honeymoon journey combining a classic safari experience with time to relax and enjoy Kenya together.

Day01

Arrival in Nairobi

Arrive in Nairobi and meet your transfer representative. Transfer to your selected hotel and enjoy time to settle in after your journey. The evening can be kept relaxed, with a special dinner arranged on request.

  • Airport meet and greet
  • Private transfer to accommodation, as arranged
  • Relaxed evening together
Day02

Nairobi to the Masai Mara

Travel from Nairobi towards the Masai Mara, enjoying the changing landscapes along the way. Arrive at your selected lodge or luxury tented camp, check in and enjoy the setting.

  • Journey to the Masai Mara
  • Check-in at selected accommodation
  • Time to relax and enjoy the surroundings
Day03

Romantic Safari in the Masai Mara

Enjoy a day of wildlife viewing in the Masai Mara with a guide. Explore the plains in search of elephants, giraffes, zebras, lions and other wildlife. A private game drive can be requested.

  • Morning game drive
  • Afternoon wildlife viewing
  • Optional private dining arrangement, subject to availability
Day04

Masai Mara to Amboseli

After breakfast, depart for the Amboseli region. This is a longer transfer day, so the final transport and routing arrangements should be planned around your selected accommodation and preferred travel style.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Transfer towards Amboseli
  • Arrive and relax at your selected lodge
Day05

Discover Amboseli

Explore Amboseli's open plains and wetland habitats, with opportunities to view elephants and other wildlife. On clear days, the landscape may offer beautiful views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Morning game drive
  • Explore Amboseli's wildlife habitats
  • Relaxed afternoon at the lodge or camp
Day06

A Day to Relax and Celebrate

Enjoy a slower day together. Depending on the selected accommodation, you may choose a spa treatment, a special meal, a scenic experience or simply time to relax and enjoy the surroundings.

  • Leisurely breakfast
  • Optional couple's experience or lodge activity
  • Special dinner arrangement on request
Day07

Return to Nairobi and Departure

After breakfast, check out and transfer back to Nairobi or to the airport according to your onward travel arrangements. Your honeymoon journey comes to an end.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Return transfer to Nairobi
  • Airport drop-off as arranged
