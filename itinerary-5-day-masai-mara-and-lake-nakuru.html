Two Remarkable Wildlife Destinations

Discover the Masai Mara and Lake Nakuru

The Masai Mara National Reserve is known for its sweeping savannah landscapes, abundant wildlife and spectacular seasonal wildebeest migration. It offers rewarding opportunities for game drives and wildlife photography.

Lake Nakuru National Park is another important wildlife destination, known for its rhinos, Rothschild's giraffes, birdlife and scenic lake surroundings.

This suggested five-day safari combines both destinations, giving you time to explore the Mara before continuing to Lake Nakuru.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A suggested five-day programme. The final route, accommodation and activities will be confirmed in your travel quotation.

Day01

Journey to the Masai Mara

Depart for the Masai Mara, travelling through the scenic Great Rift Valley. Continue towards the reserve, enjoying the changing landscapes along the way. After arrival and check-in, enjoy an afternoon game drive if time and park arrangements allow.

  • Departure for the Masai Mara
  • Scenic journey through the Rift Valley
  • Arrival and check-in at selected accommodation
  • Afternoon game drive, subject to arrival time
Day02

Full-Day Masai Mara Game Drives

Spend the day exploring the Masai Mara's open plains and wildlife habitats. Enjoy game drives in search of elephants, lions, leopards, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and other wildlife. Picnic arrangements can be made where included in your package.

  • Early breakfast
  • Morning and afternoon game drives
  • Wildlife viewing and photography
  • Return to accommodation for dinner and overnight stay
Day03

Morning Safari & Transfer to Lake Nakuru Area

Enjoy a morning game drive in the Masai Mara before breakfast or departure, according to the agreed schedule. Check out and travel towards the Lake Nakuru area, passing through scenic countryside and the Great Rift Valley region.

  • Morning game drive, subject to arrangements
  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Transfer towards Lake Nakuru
  • Check in at selected accommodation
Day04

Lake Nakuru National Park Safari

Explore Lake Nakuru National Park on a game drive through its diverse habitats. Look out for rhinos, Rothschild's giraffes, buffaloes, zebras and other wildlife. Enjoy the park's scenic surroundings and birdlife before returning to your accommodation.

  • Breakfast at your accommodation
  • Game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park
  • Wildlife and birdlife viewing
  • Return to accommodation for the evening
Day05

Breakfast & Return Journey

Enjoy breakfast and check out of your accommodation. Begin the return journey to your agreed drop-off point, bringing your five-day safari to an end.

  • Breakfast at the accommodation
  • Check-out and departure
  • Return transfer to your agreed destination
  • End of safari arrangements
