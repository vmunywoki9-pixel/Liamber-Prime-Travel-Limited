Your journey

Suggested 4-Day Itinerary

A sample programme that can be tailored to your preferred travel dates and accommodation arrangements.

Day01
Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Journey to the Mara

Depart Nairobi and travel towards the Maasai Mara through the Great Rift Valley region. Enjoy the changing scenery along the way. Arrive at your selected accommodation and settle in. Depending on arrival time, an introductory game-viewing activity may be arranged.

Day02
Maasai Mara

Explore the Mara Plains

Spend the day discovering the Mara ecosystem. Explore the open grasslands and wildlife habitats, with opportunities to encounter a variety of animals in their natural environment.

Day03
Maasai Mara

Another Day of Wildlife Exploration

Enjoy another day in the Mara, allowing more time to discover different areas and appreciate the reserve's scenery. Optional activities can be discussed and included in your personalised programme where available.

Day04
Maasai Mara to Nairobi

Return to Nairobi

After breakfast and any activities included in the confirmed schedule, begin your return journey to Nairobi. Arrive in the city, marking the end of your four-day Maasai Mara safari.

Important itinerary note: This is a suggested itinerary, not a confirmed departure schedule. Accommodation, meals, park fees, transport, game drives and other inclusions will be specified in your personalised quotation.
