More ways to discover Kenya

Explore destinations according to the kind of experience you would like to enjoy.

Wildlife & Safaris

Explore national parks and wildlife areas, including Tsavo, Amboseli, Maasai Mara and Taita Hills. Game drives and sightings depend on the itinerary and natural conditions.

Marine & Ocean Experiences

Discover coastal marine environments around Watamu, Mida Creek, Malindi, Mombasa and the South Coast. Snorkeling and boating depend on weather and sea conditions.

Culture & Heritage

Explore Swahili coastal heritage, historic settlements, local markets and community experiences around Malindi, Mombasa, Watamu, Kilifi and Lamu.

Nature & Conservation

Discover natural landscapes such as Arabuko Sokoke Forest, Shimba Hills and coastal forest environments, with nature walks and wildlife observation where available.

Adventure & Exploration

Explore distinctive landscapes and outdoor experiences, including Marafa Hell's Kitchen, Mambrui Sand Dunes and selected coastal excursions.

Beach & Relaxation

Enjoy the Kenyan coastline, from Diani's white sands to the beaches of Watamu and Malindi, with time for relaxation and optional activities.
