Two Distinctive Landscapes, One Journey

Samburu offers a striking contrast to Kenya's southern safari destinations, with semi-arid scenery, riverine forests and wildlife adapted to the northern environment.

The wider Mount Kenya region adds dramatic mountain scenery, green foothills and a different atmosphere to the journey.

This suggested programme combines wildlife exploration in Samburu with time in the Mount Kenya region. The exact accommodation and activities can be tailored to your preferences.

Planning note: The itinerary is a suggested route. The final order of destinations, accommodation, travel times and activities will be confirmed in your quotation.
Your Safari Journey

4-Day Suggested Itinerary

Travel north from Nairobi and experience Samburu's wildlife country alongside the scenery of the Mount Kenya region.

Day01

Nairobi to Samburu

Depart Nairobi and travel north through changing landscapes towards the Samburu region. Arrive at your selected lodge or camp, check in and enjoy time to relax. Depending on arrival time and the confirmed programme, an afternoon activity may be arranged.

  • Departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic journey towards Samburu
  • Arrival and accommodation check-in
Day02

Explore Samburu National Reserve

Spend the day discovering Samburu's varied habitats and wildlife. Look out for species associated with northern Kenya, including Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, gerenuk and Somali ostrich, alongside elephants and other wildlife.

  • Morning game drive
  • Explore the reserve's riverine and open habitats
  • Afternoon wildlife viewing
Day03

Samburu to the Mount Kenya Region

After breakfast, depart Samburu and travel towards the Mount Kenya region. Enjoy the changing scenery as the landscape transitions from the northern drylands to greener highland surroundings. Arrive at your selected accommodation and relax.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Journey towards the Mount Kenya region
  • Scenic stops where appropriate
Day04

Mount Kenya Region to Nairobi

Enjoy breakfast and some time to appreciate the surrounding highland scenery. Begin the return journey to Nairobi, arriving at your agreed drop-off location and bringing your four-day safari to an end.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Enjoy views of the Mount Kenya region, weather permitting
  • Return transfer to Nairobi
