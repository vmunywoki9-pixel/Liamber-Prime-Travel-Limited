The experience

A Day in the Wild at Tsavo East

Tsavo East is known for its expansive semi-arid landscapes, open plains and diverse wildlife. This short safari is designed for travellers who have limited time but would still like to experience Kenya's wilderness.

Enjoy a guided game drive through selected areas of the park, looking out for elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes and other wildlife. Sightings depend on natural conditions and animal movement.

Your safari schedule

Suggested One-Day Itinerary

The schedule below is indicative. Exact departure and return times depend on your pickup location, park entry arrangements and road conditions.

EARLY MORNING

Pickup and Journey to Tsavo East

Meet your driver-guide at the agreed pickup point and travel towards Tsavo East National Park. The departure point and transfer time will be confirmed during booking.

MORNING

Enter the Park and Begin the Game Drive

After park entry formalities, begin exploring the park's tracks and open landscapes. Look out for elephants, zebras, giraffes, buffaloes and other wildlife.

MIDDAY

Lunch and Relaxation

Take a scheduled lunch break at an appropriate designated location or facility, subject to the confirmed package arrangements.

AFTERNOON

Afternoon Wildlife Viewing

Continue the game drive through selected sections of the park. Enjoy more opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography before leaving the reserve.

LATE AFTERNOON

Exit the Park and Return Transfer

Exit the park and return to your agreed drop-off point, bringing your Tsavo Express experience to an end.

Important Safari Information

  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Camera or smartphone
  • Personal drinking water
  • Comfortable walking shoes

Booking and Travel Notes
