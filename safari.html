Frequently asked questions

Kenya Safari Information

Some useful information when planning your safari with Liamber Prime Travels Limited.

Where do Kenya safaris with Liamber Prime Travels begin?

The Kenya safari itineraries on this page are planned to begin in Nairobi and return to Nairobi at the end of the journey.

Can I request a private safari?

You can enquire about a private safari. Share your group size, dates and preferred destinations so that the arrangements can be discussed.

Can I combine more than one national park?

Yes. You can enquire about a multi-destination itinerary combining selected parks and other travel experiences.

How much does a Kenya safari cost?

Safari costs depend on the destination, duration, number of travellers, transport arrangements and accommodation category. Contact us for a proposal based on your requirements.

Can I customise my safari itinerary?

Yes. You can share your travel preferences and enquire about a tailor-made itinerary.

How do I request a safari quotation?

Use the contact page or reach us through WhatsApp or email. Include your travel dates, group size and preferred destination.
