See the Mara differently

A Safari Above the African Plains

The Maasai Mara is renowned for its open grasslands, wildlife and remarkable natural scenery. This suggested three-day experience combines a traditional safari with the possibility of a hot-air balloon flight.

Take in the landscape from a new perspective and enjoy time exploring the Mara's wildlife habitats.

  • Travel from Nairobi to the Maasai Mara
  • Experience game viewing in the Mara ecosystem
  • Option to arrange a hot-air balloon flight
  • Enjoy scenic views and photography opportunities
  • Return to Nairobi at the end of the safari

The balloon experience

Float Above the Mara

A hot-air balloon flight offers a remarkable aerial perspective of the Maasai Mara's landscapes. Depending on the operator and confirmed arrangements, the experience may begin around sunrise.

Watch the plains come alive from above and appreciate the scale of this celebrated wildlife ecosystem.

Important: Balloon flights are operated by specialist providers and are subject to availability, weather and safety conditions. The flight and any associated extras must be confirmed in your quotation.

Your journey

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A sample programme that can be customised around balloon flight availability and your travel preferences.

Day01
Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Journey to the Mara

Depart Nairobi and travel towards the Maasai Mara through the Great Rift Valley region. Arrive at your selected accommodation and settle in. Depending on arrival time and the confirmed programme, enjoy an introductory game-viewing experience.

Day02
Maasai Mara

Balloon Safari and Wildlife Exploration

If booked and confirmed, begin the day with a hot-air balloon flight, subject to weather and operator arrangements. Continue with the day's planned wildlife viewing and enjoy the remarkable scenery of the Mara.

Day03
Maasai Mara to Nairobi

Farewell to the Mara

Enjoy your final moments in the Mara according to the confirmed schedule, then begin the return journey to Nairobi. Arrive in Nairobi, marking the end of your three-day safari.

Planning note: The balloon flight is not automatically included in the safari price. It must be requested and confirmed separately in the final quotation. Flight availability, departure time, operator requirements and weather conditions may affect the programme.
