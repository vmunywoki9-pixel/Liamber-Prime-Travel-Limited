Discover Amboseli

A Safari Beneath the Great Mountain

Amboseli National Park is celebrated for its large elephant herds, expansive savannah landscapes and spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro when weather conditions are clear.

This sample three-day journey combines scenic travel, guided game drives and time to appreciate the park's wildlife and natural beauty.

Your Journey

Sample Day-by-Day Itinerary

A suggested programme that can be adjusted to your travel dates, accommodation preference and budget.

Day01

Nairobi to Amboseli National Park

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel towards Amboseli through the scenic landscapes of southern Kenya. Arrive at your selected lodge or camp in time for lunch, depending on the travel schedule.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi.
  • Arrival and check-in at the selected accommodation.
  • Afternoon game drive in Amboseli.
  • Return to the lodge for dinner and overnight stay.
Day02

Full-Day Amboseli Wildlife Exploration

Spend the day discovering the park's varied habitats and wildlife. Amboseli is particularly well known for its elephant population and open landscapes.

  • Early morning game drive.
  • Explore open plains, swamps and wildlife areas.
  • Look out for elephants, zebras, giraffes, buffaloes and birdlife.
  • Enjoy lunch according to the selected safari arrangement.
  • Afternoon wildlife viewing before returning to camp.
Day03

Morning Game Drive and Return to Nairobi

Enjoy breakfast and, depending on the departure schedule, an early morning game drive before checking out and beginning the journey back to Nairobi.

  • Breakfast at the lodge or camp.
  • Optional morning game drive, subject to timing.
  • Check-out and depart Amboseli.
  • Arrive in Nairobi, marking the end of the safari.
