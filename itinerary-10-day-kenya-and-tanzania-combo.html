Your East African journey

Suggested 10-Day Itinerary

A sample route from Nairobi through Kenya and into northern Tanzania. The final route is subject to confirmation with the travel operator and border arrangements.

01
Nairobi, Kenya

Arrival and Nairobi Welcome

Arrive in Nairobi or begin your journey from the city. Meet your travel representative, review the programme and prepare for the safari adventure ahead.

02
Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Journey to the Maasai Mara

Travel from Nairobi towards the Maasai Mara. Arrive at your selected accommodation and, depending on arrival time, enjoy an afternoon game drive.

03
Maasai Mara, Kenya

Full Day in the Mara

Explore the reserve on game drives, looking out for elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes and big cats. Wildlife sightings vary depending on season and conditions.

04
Maasai Mara, Kenya

Another Day of Wildlife Discovery

Continue exploring the Mara's wildlife habitats and landscapes. Enjoy further game-viewing opportunities and time to appreciate the reserve.

05
Kenya to Tanzania

Cross-Border Journey to Tanzania

Travel towards the agreed Kenya–Tanzania border crossing. Complete the required immigration and customs procedures before continuing to your confirmed overnight destination in Tanzania.

06
Northern Tanzania

Explore the Tanzania Safari Region

Begin exploring the selected Tanzania safari area. The exact destination and game-drive schedule will depend on the confirmed route, accommodation and park arrangements.

07
Tanzania

Full Day of Safari Experiences

Enjoy a day of wildlife viewing and scenery in the selected national park or conservation area. Your guide can help plan the day around local conditions and park regulations.

08
Tanzania

Discover More of Tanzania

Continue your Tanzania experience with a further day for wildlife viewing or a planned activity in the selected destination.

09
Tanzania to Kenya

Return Journey Towards Kenya

Begin the return journey towards the agreed border crossing. Complete the necessary border formalities and continue to the confirmed overnight stop.

10
Kenya

Conclude Your East African Safari

Continue to Nairobi or the agreed departure point, depending on your travel arrangements. Conclude your journey with memorable experiences from two East African countries.

Important cross-border planning note: This is a preliminary itinerary concept. The Tanzania destinations, border crossing, transport provider, guide arrangements, immigration requirements, park fees and accommodation have not been specified in the source package listing. They must all be confirmed before the safari is sold or booked. Cross-border vehicle and guide arrangements may require separate operators or approved arrangements.
