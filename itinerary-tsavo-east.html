Discover Tsavo East

A Journey Into Kenya's Wild Heart

Tsavo East National Park is known for its expansive open plains, striking landscapes and the famous red-dusted elephants. Its wilderness offers opportunities to encounter a variety of wildlife in their natural habitat.

This suggested three-day safari combines scenic travel, guided game drives and time to enjoy the atmosphere of the African bush.

Your Safari Experience

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A suggested itinerary that can be adjusted to suit your travel dates, accommodation preference and departure location.

Day01

Travel to Tsavo East & Afternoon Game Drive

Depart for Tsavo East National Park, enjoying the changing scenery along the way. Arrive at the park and proceed for an afternoon game drive, exploring the open plains in search of elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes and other wildlife.

  • Scenic journey to Tsavo East
  • Park entry and afternoon game drive
  • Check-in at your selected safari accommodation
Day02

Full-Day Wildlife Exploration

Enjoy an early morning game drive when wildlife is often active. After breakfast, continue exploring the park, with opportunities to visit areas around the Galana River and Aruba Dam depending on the route and conditions.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast and further park exploration
  • Wildlife viewing and photography
  • Afternoon game drive and return to the lodge
Day03

Morning Game Drive & Return Journey

Start the day with a final morning game drive, taking in the park's landscapes and looking out for wildlife. Return to the lodge for breakfast, check out and begin your journey back to your agreed drop-off point.

  • Morning game drive
  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Return transfer
