Experience the Contrasting Landscapes of Tsavo

Tsavo East and Tsavo West form part of Kenya's extensive Tsavo ecosystem. Together, they offer varied scenery, wildlife habitats and memorable safari experiences.

Tsavo East is known for its expansive plains and red-dusted elephants. Tsavo West offers a more varied landscape, with rugged hills, volcanic features and natural springs.

This suggested three-day itinerary combines both parks, with game drives and overnight accommodation arranged according to your selected package.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A suggested route combining Tsavo East and Tsavo West. The order of visits may be adjusted depending on your departure point, accommodation and travel arrangements.

Day01

Journey to Tsavo East & Afternoon Game Drive

Depart for Tsavo East National Park. After arrival and park entry, enjoy an afternoon game drive across the park's open landscapes. Look out for elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes and other wildlife.

  • Travel to Tsavo East
  • Park entry and afternoon game drive
  • Wildlife viewing and photography
  • Check in at your selected accommodation
Day02

Morning Game Drive & Transfer to Tsavo West

Start with an early morning game drive in Tsavo East. After breakfast, depart for Tsavo West, enjoying the changing scenery along the way. Depending on the route and time available, explore selected park areas before proceeding to your accommodation.

  • Early morning game drive in Tsavo East
  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Transfer towards Tsavo West
  • Afternoon wildlife viewing and lodge check-in
Day03

Explore Tsavo West & Return Journey

Enjoy a morning game drive in Tsavo West. Subject to time, park conditions and the agreed route, you may visit the Mzima Springs area. Later, return to the lodge for breakfast or proceed with the planned departure arrangements before travelling back to your drop-off point.

  • Morning game drive in Tsavo West
  • Optional visit to Mzima Springs, subject to arrangements
  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Return transfer to your agreed destination
