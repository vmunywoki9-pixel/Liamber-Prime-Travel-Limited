Two Remarkable Safari Destinations

From Amboseli's Open Plains to Tsavo's Wilderness

Amboseli National Park is renowned for its large elephant herds and striking views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Its open landscapes provide wonderful opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography.

Tsavo offers a different safari atmosphere, with vast wilderness, varied habitats and the famous red-dusted elephants of the region.

This suggested four-day itinerary combines the two destinations into one journey. The route and accommodation can be adjusted to suit your departure point and travel preferences.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A suggested four-day route. The final sequence and park visits will depend on your departure point, accommodation and confirmed arrangements.

Day01

Journey to Amboseli National Park

Depart for Amboseli National Park. On arrival, proceed for an afternoon game drive across the park's open plains. Enjoy opportunities to see elephants, zebras, giraffes, buffaloes and other wildlife, with views of Mount Kilimanjaro when weather conditions allow.

  • Travel to Amboseli
  • Park entry and afternoon game drive
  • Wildlife viewing and photography
  • Check in at your selected accommodation
Day02

Explore Amboseli & Transfer Towards Tsavo

Enjoy an early morning game drive when wildlife is often active. Return for breakfast, check out and continue towards the Tsavo region according to the confirmed route. Enjoy the changing landscapes during the journey before settling into your accommodation.

  • Early morning game drive in Amboseli
  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Transfer towards the selected Tsavo park area
  • Overnight at your selected accommodation
Day03

Tsavo Wildlife & Scenic Game Drives

Spend the day exploring the agreed Tsavo park area. Enjoy game drives through the wilderness in search of elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras, antelopes and other wildlife. The exact park and game-drive route will depend on the package selected.

  • Breakfast at your accommodation
  • Game drives in the selected Tsavo park
  • Wildlife viewing and photography
  • Return to accommodation for the evening
Day04

Morning Game Drive & Return Journey

Enjoy breakfast and, depending on your departure schedule, an optional morning game drive. Check out and begin the return journey to your agreed drop-off point.

  • Breakfast at the accommodation
  • Optional morning game drive, subject to arrangements
  • Check-out and departure
  • Return transfer to your agreed destination
