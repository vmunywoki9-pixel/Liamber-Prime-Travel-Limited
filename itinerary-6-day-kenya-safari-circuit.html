The Safari Experience

Six Days of Kenyan Adventure

This six-day safari circuit is designed to introduce travellers to the diversity of Kenya's natural attractions. From open savannahs to scenic landscapes, each destination offers a different perspective of the country's wildlife and beauty.

Travel between destinations with opportunities for game drives, photography, relaxation and appreciation of Kenya's natural heritage. The route and accommodation can be tailored to your travel dates and preferences.

Your Journey

6-Day Safari Itinerary

A suggested itinerary for your Kenyan safari. The final route, parks and accommodation will be confirmed in your personalised quotation.

Day 01

Nairobi – Amboseli National Park

Depart Nairobi for Amboseli National Park, travelling through Kenya's scenic countryside. Arrive in the Amboseli region and proceed with the day's planned safari activities. Enjoy views of the expansive plains and, weather permitting, the spectacular backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Overnight: Amboseli area Safari activities
Day 02

Explore Amboseli National Park

Spend the day exploring Amboseli's wildlife habitats. Look out for elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and a variety of birdlife. Enjoy game-viewing opportunities across the park and take in its beautiful natural scenery.

Overnight: Amboseli area Wildlife photography
Day 03

Amboseli – Tsavo West National Park

Depart Amboseli and travel towards Tsavo West. Discover a landscape of rugged hills, open plains and diverse habitats. Depending on the confirmed programme, enjoy a game drive and opportunities to explore the park's natural attractions.

Overnight: Tsavo West area Scenic transfer
Day 04

Tsavo West – Tsavo East National Park

Continue your safari towards Tsavo East. Enjoy the changing landscapes and the chance to spot wildlife along the way. Tsavo East is known for its vast open spaces, red-earth scenery and large elephant herds.

Overnight: Tsavo East area Game viewing
Day 05

Discover Tsavo East National Park

Enjoy further exploration of Tsavo East, with opportunities to see elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes and other wildlife. Experience the park's distinctive landscapes and enjoy time for photography and relaxation.

Overnight: Tsavo East area Wildlife photography
Day 06

Tsavo East – Nairobi

After breakfast, depart for Nairobi. Enjoy the scenery along the return journey and arrive in the city, marking the end of your six-day Kenyan safari adventure.

Safari concludes Nairobi

This is a sample route for presentation purposes, not a confirmed booking schedule. Actual park visits, travel times, overnight locations and activities depend on the final itinerary and availability.

What to Pack

Prepare for changing weather conditions and outdoor safari activities. We recommend packing light and bringing essentials for comfortable travel.

  • Comfortable neutral-coloured clothing
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Camera and spare batteries
  • Personal medication and toiletries
  • Light jacket for cool mornings
