The Mara Experience

Follow the Story of the Wild

The Masai Mara is renowned for its open grasslands, diverse wildlife and seasonal movement of wildebeest and other grazing animals across the wider Mara-Serengeti ecosystem.

This five-day safari concept allows time to explore different parts of the reserve, enjoy guided game drives and appreciate the remarkable landscapes of the Mara.

Depending on your travel dates, you may have an opportunity to witness parts of the Great Migration. Wildlife movements are natural and sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Travel note: The itinerary below is a suggested programme. Final routing, accommodation, transport and timings will be confirmed during your booking.
Your Safari Journey

5-Day Suggested Itinerary

A five-day journey designed to give you time in the Masai Mara, with opportunities for wildlife viewing and relaxation.

Day01

Nairobi to the Masai Mara

Depart Nairobi for the Masai Mara, travelling through Kenya's scenic countryside and the Great Rift Valley region. Arrive in the Mara area, check in at your selected accommodation and enjoy time to settle in.

  • Departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic journey towards the Mara
  • Arrival, check-in and relaxation
Day02

Full-Day Masai Mara Game Drive

Spend the day exploring the reserve with a professional guide. Look out for elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes, big cats and other wildlife across the open plains.

  • Morning and afternoon wildlife viewing
  • Explore different habitats and plains
  • Picnic lunch may be arranged during the day
Day03

Migration and Wildlife Exploration

Continue exploring the Mara in search of wildlife activity. When seasonal conditions bring migrating herds into the area, your guide can plan the day's route around reported wildlife movements.

  • Extended game-viewing opportunities
  • Look for wildebeest, zebras and predators
  • Learn about the Mara ecosystem
Day04

Another Day in the Mara

Enjoy another day in the reserve, visiting areas that offer different landscapes and wildlife habitats. Your guide will help adapt the game-drive plan to the day's conditions and recent sightings.

  • Morning wildlife viewing
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Return to the lodge or camp
Day05

Masai Mara to Nairobi

After breakfast, check out and begin the return journey to Nairobi. Arrive in the city, marking the end of your five-day safari.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Return journey to Nairobi
  • Drop-off at an agreed location

When is the Great Migration?

The wildebeest migration moves through the wider Serengeti-Mara ecosystem at different times of the year. Large herds are often associated with the Masai Mara around the middle and later part of the year, but exact timing varies. River crossings are unpredictable, and no safari operator can guarantee a crossing or a particular wildlife sighting.
