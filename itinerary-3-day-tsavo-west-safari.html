The Safari Experience

Explore Kenya's Scenic Wilderness

Tsavo West National Park is known for its dramatic scenery, rolling hills, volcanic landscapes and diverse wildlife. It offers a distinctive safari experience for travellers looking to explore Kenya beyond the familiar.

During this three-day journey, discover the park's natural beauty and enjoy opportunities to encounter elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and a variety of birdlife.

Your Journey

3-Day Tsavo West Safari Itinerary

Follow this suggested programme through Tsavo West. Your final itinerary will be arranged around your travel dates, accommodation and preferred activities.

Day01

Nairobi – Tsavo West National Park

Depart Nairobi and travel towards Tsavo West National Park. Enjoy the changing countryside as you approach the park. On arrival, proceed with the planned safari activities and enjoy your first opportunity to explore the wilderness.

Overnight: Tsavo West area Safari activities
Day02

Full-Day Tsavo West Exploration

Spend the day discovering Tsavo West's diverse landscapes and wildlife habitats. Enjoy game-viewing opportunities as you look out for elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and other wildlife. Visit attractions included in your confirmed safari programme.

Overnight: Tsavo West area Wildlife photography
Day03

Tsavo West – Nairobi

Enjoy breakfast and, where time permits, a final morning safari activity. Afterwards, depart for Nairobi and enjoy the scenery along the return journey. Arrive in Nairobi marking the end of your three-day safari.

Safari concludes Nairobi

This is a sample itinerary. Travel times, park activities, overnight locations and attraction visits will depend on the confirmed route, accommodation and booking arrangements.
