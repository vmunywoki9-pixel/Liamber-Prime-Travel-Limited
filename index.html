Plan Kenya Safaris and Holidays with Liamber Prime Travels

Kenya is one of Africa's remarkable travel destinations, offering wildlife-rich national parks, expansive savannahs, mountain scenery, vibrant cultures and beautiful Indian Ocean beaches. Whether you are visiting for a wildlife safari, a family holiday, a romantic escape or a cultural experience, Kenya offers a variety of journeys to discover.

Liamber Prime Travels Limited helps travellers explore Kenya through safari experiences, holiday packages and tailor-made travel arrangements. Our journeys can include destinations such as the Maasai Mara, Amboseli National Park, Tsavo, Lake Nakuru and Samburu.

Kenya wildlife safaris

A Kenya safari provides opportunities to experience wildlife and landscapes across different parks and reserves. Game drives, scenic journeys and time spent in the natural environment can make each destination distinctive. Explore our Kenya safari packages to find a journey that suits your available time.

Kenya beach holidays and coastal experiences

Kenya's coast offers a different pace of travel, with beaches, ocean experiences and coastal culture. Discover our beach holiday options and consider combining a wildlife safari with time at the coast.

Tailor-made tours from Nairobi

Nairobi provides a convenient starting point for many Kenya safari itineraries. Share your preferred travel dates, destinations and group size with our team. We can discuss a suitable route and prepare a proposed itinerary for your review. Visit our contact page to begin planning.
