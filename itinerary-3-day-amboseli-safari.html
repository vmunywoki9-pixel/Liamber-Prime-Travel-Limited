Experience Amboseli's Wild Beauty

Amboseli National Park offers an unforgettable combination of wildlife, open landscapes and mountain scenery. It is especially known for its elephant herds and views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

This suggested three-day safari allows you to explore the park at a relaxed pace while enjoying the atmosphere of the Kenyan wilderness.

  • Travel from Nairobi to Amboseli
  • Explore Amboseli's wildlife habitats
  • Look out for the park's elephant herds
  • Enjoy views of Mount Kilimanjaro when visible
  • Return to Nairobi after the safari

Your journey

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A sample programme that can be adjusted to your travel dates and accommodation preferences.

Day01
Nairobi to Amboseli

Journey to Amboseli National Park

Depart Nairobi and travel towards Amboseli. Enjoy the changing scenery along the way before arriving at your selected accommodation. Depending on arrival time and the confirmed programme, an introductory game drive may be arranged.

Day02
Amboseli National Park

Explore Amboseli's Wildlife

Spend the day exploring the park's diverse habitats. Look out for elephants and other wildlife, and enjoy the scenic views of Mount Kilimanjaro when conditions are favourable. Your game-viewing schedule will follow the confirmed safari programme.

Day03
Amboseli to Nairobi

Farewell to Amboseli

Enjoy your final moments in the Amboseli area according to the confirmed schedule. Begin the return journey to Nairobi, bringing your three-day safari to an end.

Important itinerary note: This is a suggested itinerary, not a confirmed departure schedule. Accommodation, meals, park fees, transport, game drives and other inclusions will be specified in your personalised quotation.
